4 Destinations
01 • UTTAR PRADESH Tap to explore ↗

Agra — Taj Mahal & Agra Fort

Witness the eternal beauty of the Taj Mahal at sunrise, a UNESCO World Heritage wonder, followed by the grandeur of the red sandstone Agra Fort.

Taj Mahal Agra Fort Mughal Heritage Mehtab Bagh
02 • RAJASTHAN Tap to explore ↗

Jaipur — Hawa Mahal & The Pink City

The historic capital of Rajputana royalty. Explore the world-famous facade of Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds), the hilltop Amer Fort, and royal heritage of Rajasthan's Pink City.

Hawa Mahal Amer Fort City Palace Jantar Mantar
03 • THAR DESERT Tap to explore ↗

Jaisalmer — Thar Desert Camel Safari

Step into the golden Thar Desert. Experience sunset camel rides and safari across the undulating Sam Sand Dunes, starlit camping, and the living Golden Fort (Sonar Qila).

Camel Safari Sam Sand Dunes Desert Camp Golden Fort
04 • RAJASTHAN Tap to explore ↗

Jodhpur — Mehrangarh Fort & The Blue City

Stand before the colossal Mehrangarh Fort perched 400 feet above the vibrant blue-roofed city, and explore the marble cenotaph of Jaswant Thada and royal palaces.

Mehrangarh Fort Blue City Lanes Jaswant Thada Umaid Bhawan

Common questions about One Last Trip

This trip is officially organized by the Department of Computer Science, MES Arts & Science College, Chathamangalam for the 2024–28 batch.

All student information is directly saved into the official Google Sheet of the trip committee, and Aadhaar PDFs are stored securely in Google Drive for travel permissions and hotel check-in authorities.
